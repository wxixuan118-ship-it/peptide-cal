Privacy Policy

Short version: the calculators run in your browser, and the numbers you type never leave your device.

Data you enter

Vial amounts, water volumes, doses and every other input are processed by JavaScript on your own device. They are not sent to our servers, stored in a database or shared with anyone.

Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard access logs — such as IP address, browser type and the page requested — for security and troubleshooting. These logs are kept for a limited time and are not sold.

Cookies and accounts

The calculators need no account and set no tracking cookies. If we ever add analytics, this page will be updated to say exactly what is collected and how to opt out.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes; the footer shows the date of the last review. Back to the peptide calculator.
